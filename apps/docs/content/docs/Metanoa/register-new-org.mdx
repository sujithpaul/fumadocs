## Overview

To register your clinic on Metanoa, visit [web.metanoa.ai/register](https://web.metanoa.ai/register) and complete the registration form. You'll need to provide your clinic name, email, and password. Once registered, you'll become the Owner of your new organization with full control to add team members, create branches, and customize settings.

## Registration Steps

1. Navigate to [web.metanoa.ai/register](https://web.metanoa.ai/register)
2. Enter your organization name (your clinic's name on Metanoa)
3. Add your email address (this becomes your login)
4. Create a password with at least 6 characters and confirm it
5. Click "Create Account" to complete registration
6. Sign in with your new email and password

<Callout title='Good to Know'>

- You become the Owner with full control over your clinic's workspace
- Each email can only be associated with one organization
- No email verification is required to start using Metanoa
- To add your name, click your organization name at the bottom left, go to Settings, then Profile

</Callout>

## Multiple Organizations

If you need to set up another clinic, you'll need to use a different email address since each email can only be associated with one organization.

## Multiple Clinic Locations

You can add as many branches as you need under one organization. After logging in, click your organization name in the top right corner to manage branches.
